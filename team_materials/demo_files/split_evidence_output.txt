EventPulse AI - train/test split evidence (synthetic data only)
========================================================================
Dataset: 783 registrations, 600 students, attend rate 67.4% (target is SIMULATED)

PART A - the split actually used (core.model_training.grouped_holdout_split)
  Train: 626 rows / 484 students (79.9%), attend rate 67.4%
  Test : 157 rows / 116 students (20.1%), attend rate 67.5%
  Students in BOTH train and test: 0  -> PASS (no leakage)
  Rows add up: 626 + 157 = 783 -> PASS

PART B - 90:10 vs 80:20 vs 67:33 (~70:30) (10 student-grouped splits each, same model)
  Split   Train rows Test rows  AUC mean  AUC spread  Acc mean  Acc spread
  90:10          704        79     0.885   0.812-0.940     80.8%   74.7%-88.6%
  80:20          626       157     0.856   0.819-0.879     80.1%   76.4%-84.7%
  67:33          522       261     0.865   0.811-0.908     81.8%   79.4%-85.1%

How to read Part B:
  * 'spread' = lowest-highest result over 10 different splits.
  * 90:10 tests on only ~79 rows, so its result swings the most (least reliable).
  * 80:20 and 67:33 give similar, stable results; neither is clearly better.
  * 80:20 keeps more rows for training while still testing on ~157 rows,
    so it is a reasonable, standard choice - not a tuned 'best' ratio.
  * Model choice itself used 5-fold group cross-validation on the training part only.
